Audience Archival – Option B

Prototype · Option B · matched to Nightly

Audiences in a hierarchy are archived only from the Hierarchy tab with Archive tree. It covers the audience you opened and every audience below it. On the listing, the checkbox and ⋯ Archive are disabled for hierarchy audiences. Standalone audiences archive as usual.
